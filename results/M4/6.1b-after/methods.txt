Step 6.1b repartition comparison, 2026-11-21

All three modes used the same MinGW release build, seed 20261119,
log level 1, tracking rows, no selected snapshots and convergence defaults.
Each of the ten main and hard datasets has 100 completed runs per mode,
including worms_2d. Runs 1-25, 26-50, 51-75 and 76-100 were executed in
alternating mode orders. The program writes its first perfect/failed example
snapshots after the timed region, even with --snapshots none.

The mode folders retain run_config.txt, per-run SSE and CI, dataset metadata,
and report tables with mean and sample SD of SSE and time, mean and relative
CI and success rate. paired-comparison.csv compares inward with both on
matching run indices and seeds. Different assignments can consume random
draws differently, so matching seeds do not imply identical random streams.
Timing differences include machine and ordering noise.

inward and inward-nonchild matched exactly on all 1000 converged runs
(run, seed, SSE, CI). The separate capped goldens demonstrate their difference
when child points are not already optimally assigned. Explicit both matched
the preceding 6.1 milestone on its nine 100-run datasets and the ten
worms_2d runs.

repartition_diagnostics.csv comes from separate log-level-3 replays of all
3000 runs; those timings were discarded. Every replay matched its level-1
run, seed, SSE and CI, and produced K-1 repartition events per run. Touched
counts include both split children even if nothing moved and therefore are
not a count of Voronoi neighbours. pass1 and pass2 are moves per split;
child is the subset of pass2 moves between children. No such child switch
occurred in these converged runs.
