Step 6.6 distinct-coordinate milestone, 2026-11-22

The runs used MinGW release, seed 20261119, log level 1, tracking rows,
no selected snapshots and convergence defaults. The five small main
datasets have 100 KM and split runs, plus one RKM and one RS run each.
worms_2d has 10 KM and split runs, except Local has 100; Global, RKM and
RS are omitted. The hard datasets have 100 KM, Random, Intra, Local and
bisecting runs, 10 Global and Global-Basic runs, and one RS run each.
Hard-set RKM was omitted as requested after a 10-repeat pilot estimated
about 2.5 hours per side at its default 1000 repeats.

The before split results reuse 6.5-after and hard-set RS reuses 6.4-after.
Main KM/RKM/RS and hard-set KM were measured immediately before this step.
All 83 before/after run files match exactly on run, seed, SSE and CI,
including all 400 hard-set KM runs. This does not imply the algorithms are
identical on data or seeds that trigger coordinate collisions.

Distinct-coordinate validation runs once per dataset outside the run
stopwatch; candidate comparisons and any fallback searches are timed.
RS full-run mean-time ratios ranged from 0.97 to 1.01, including timing
noise; the reported last-improvement time is retrospective.
The 15 changed golden lines are listed in golden-changes.txt.
