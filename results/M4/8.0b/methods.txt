State and RNG verification
--------------------------
For each of unlimited iterations and caps 1 and 2, the old and new MinGW
binaries ran all four SSE-split variants five times on g2-1-10 and g2-1-20
(K=2), s1 and s4 (K=15), and generated e04_tiny and e07_k2 (K=2). Tracking,
inner traces and snapshots at counts 1-2 and 14-15 were enabled. All 72 run
files and 360 run/seed/SSE/CI rows match; 13924 tracking and iteration-stat
rows match excluding time, and 2500 centroid/partition snapshots match
byte-for-byte. The e04 case includes a singleton child.

With the same three iteration settings, the MSVC binary matches the old
MinGW binary on all 120 g2 runs, 1098 tracking rows excluding time and 588
snapshots. On decimal-coordinate worms_2d (K=35), one uncapped run of each
variant matches the old MinGW binary on run/seed/SSE/CI, 5561 tracking rows
excluding time and 26 snapshots, for both new MinGW and MSVC binaries.

input-hashes.csv lists the five real input triples and the two generated
fixtures. The 15 real files match the 6.7 hashes before and after the
comparisons. Generated-fixture hashes were recorded after the comparisons;
their deterministic generator is tests/gen_data.c.

The focused unit test constructs the first split and final k-means without
trials, checking SSE, CI, labels, centroids and the exact PCG state for all
four variants, all three Local repartition modes, and capped and uncapped
iterations. It fails on the old implementation because the old K=2 trials
advance the RNG. A K=3 trial-selection test also verifies the unchanged RNG
state after its necessary evaluations. Each run in experiment.c seeds its own
RNG, and the K=2 path runs only deterministic refinement and tracking after
the first split. Debug level 3 therefore no longer prints the unused trial
iterations at K=2; recorded inner traces and snapshots still match.
