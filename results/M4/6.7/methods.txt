This reads the individual summary.csv and run CSV files, verifies matching

  derived-comparison.csv    Labeled report rows with source/build provenance.
  paired-local.csv          Inward versus both on all 23 datasets.

builds, so they are not paired. The tables do not combine or average runs
across configurations or builds. Timings remain individual report values;
no cross-configuration timing ratios are computed. Paired seeds can lead
to different subsequent random draws after algorithm states diverge.
